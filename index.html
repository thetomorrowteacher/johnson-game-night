House rules

Flaudjers

Two Flaudjers kicks in only when 6 or more people play.

  1. Everyone looks at their secret card. The Flaudjer's card has no word.
  2. Go around the table. Everyone says one word that fits the secret.
  3. Talk it over, then vote out the player you think is the Flaudjer.
  4. A caught Flaudjer gets one guess at the word to steal the round.

Clue round

One word each, in this order. Not too obvious, not too vague.

    Last chance

    Scoreboard

    Flaudjer escapes: +2. Flaudjer caught but guesses the word: +1. Flaudjer caught and misses: +1 to everyone else.
