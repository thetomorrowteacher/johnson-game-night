Fakers

Two fakers kicks in only when 6 or more people play.

  1. Everyone looks at their secret card. The faker’s card has no word.
  2. Go around the table. Everyone says one word that fits the secret.
  3. Talk it over, then vote out the player you think is flaugin’.
  4. A caught faker gets one guess at the word to steal the round.
