Family Game Night

Pick a game for the TV, then everyone joins on their own phone.

Tonight's games

One of you is flaugin’

Flaugin

Flaugin’ say it: FLAW-jin (adj.) Memphis slang for lying, fronting, faking it. A Flauger (FLAW-jer) is the one doing it.

Everyone gets the secret word except whoever’s flaugin’. Give one-word clues, sniff out the Flauger, and vote them out. 3 to 30 players.

Somebody’s mission is different

Parrot

Everyone gets a secret mission for the debate on the TV, except the Parrot, whose mission is a little different. The Parrot has to parrot everyone else and act natural. Find them and vote them out. 3 to 30 players.

Somebody’s tweakin’

Tweakin

Tweakin’ say it: TWEE-kin Black American slang for acting off or getting it wrong.

Everyone reads the same case file, but one file has two details changed, and nobody knows whose. Ask questions, compare stories, and catch who’s tweakin’. 3 to 30 players.

Everyone wants something

Secret Agenda

The family has to make a decision. Everyone has a secret goal: get the beach picked, keep pizza off the table, make two people change their minds. Negotiate, then vote. 3 to 30 players.

Crack the secret rule

RuleBreakers

The gate only lets certain words or numbers through. Test your guesses on your phone, watch what gets ACCEPTED, and be first to crack the rule. 2 to 30 players.

Say it without saying it

Talmbout

Talmbout say it: TALM-bout as in “what you talmbout?”

Team Red vs. Team Green. Describe the word on your phone without saying it or any banned word, while the other team watches your card and hits BUZZ. 4 to 30 players.

Read the room

Vibe Check

One player sees a secret spot on a dial, like Cold to Hot, and gives a single clue. Everyone slides their guess on their phone, then the TV spins the reveal. 3 to 30 players.

Somebody in this house is an Opp

Man Gone

Bush crack, man gone Bahamian for “when trouble starts, run.”

Secret roles on every phone. At night the Opps snatch somebody, Big Mama keeps someone safe, and the Nosy Neighbor peeks in a window. By day, talk it out and vote. 5 to 30 players.

Big Joker, Little Joker, deuce

Spades

The cookout classic. Four phones, partners across, bid your books and run the table. House rules: Big Joker, Little Joker, then the 2♠ are the top spades. 4 players (others can watch).

Pass the pain

Stack

The cookout card game. Match colors and numbers, stack +2s and +4s until somebody breaks, and drop doubles and triples. The TV holds the piles; your hand stays on your phone. 2 to 10 players.

5 locks. 1 clock.

Locked In

Locked in brain rot for focused. You'll need to be.

A family escape room. Every phone gets different secret clues, so you have to talk and put the pieces together to crack 5 locks before time runs out. Three rooms: Big Mama's Kitchen, the Memphis Studio and Nassau Treasure. 2 to 30 players.

Flaugers

Two Flaugers kicks in only when 6 or more people play.

  1. Everyone looks at their secret card. The Flauger’s card has no word.
  2. Go around the table. Everyone says one word that fits the secret.
  3. Talk it over, then vote out the player you think is flaugin’.
  4. A caught Flauger gets one guess at the word to steal the round.
